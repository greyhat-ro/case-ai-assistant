Sanctions List Hit Handling — BIS Entity List

Demo SOP for verifying and logging a hit against the BIS Entity List.

  1. Verify the hit against the live BIS Entity List export, using name and address, not name alone.
  2. Check for a partial-match false positive before treating the hit as confirmed.
  3. Log a confirmed hit as a Positive Match Record; log a cleared false positive with the reviewer's name and reasoning.
  4. Escalate to the sanctions lead if the counterparty appears on more than one list.
